# MAGGO · The Architecture of Continuity

A high-fashion editorial publication and luxury portfolio for **MAGGO**, founded by Mishita Maggo.

## Overview
MAGGO synthesizes 800 years of regional Indian handcraft intelligence—Bengal Kantha, Kutch needlework, Odisha Pattachitra, Andhra Kalamkari, and Mithila Madhubani—with clean European architectural tailoring and sculptural jewellery.

## Structure
- `/` - The Editorial Cover Story & Selected Highlights
- `/story` - Founder's Monograph & Dialogue
- `/designs` - The Curated Couture, Indie Office Waistcoats, Tailored Blazers, and Artisan Shirts
- `/journal` - Editorial Archive & Studio Dispatches (Coming Soon)
- `/inquiries` - Direct Editorial & Design Inquiries

## Development
Run a local static server:
```bash
npx serve -p 3000 .
```
Or open `index.html` directly in any modern browser.
